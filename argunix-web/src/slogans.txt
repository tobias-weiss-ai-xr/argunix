# Header slogans — one per line. A random one shows on every page load.
# Blank lines and lines starting with `#` are ignored.

# --- Forge independence ---
Your CI is too good to be locked in a forge.
One CI. Every forge.
Switch forges any day — keep your CI.
Host projects anywhere, get the same CI everywhere.
Your CI shouldn't care which forge you use.
Forge-specific CI is a forge-specific cage.
CI that outlives your choice of forge.

# --- Reproducible: no more single source of truth ---
Works on your machine? Then it works in CI.
No more commit-push-wait-hope.
Run the exact CI build on your own laptop.
The CI shouldn't be the only place your build works.
Same sandbox on your laptop and in CI.
Stop debugging the CI - reproduce it locally.

# --- Less complexity ---
CI without the thousand-line YAML.
Your CI config shouldn't outgrow your code.
Describe your build once, in Nix - not in CI.

# --- Speed & caching ---
Nix-cached builds: most jobs finish in a second.
Caching that just works — because Nix already did it.
